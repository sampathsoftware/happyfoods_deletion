Account Deletion Request — Happy Foods

Effective Date: July 24, 2026

This page explains how users of the Happy Foods app (published by Happy Foods) can request deletion of their account and associated personal data, and what happens to your data once a deletion request is processed.

How to Request Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your Happy Foods account in either of the following ways:

Option 1: Request via Email

  1. Send an email to support@happyfoods.com from the email address or phone number registered with your account.
  2. Use the subject line: "Account Deletion Request".
  3. Include your registered mobile number and/or email address so we can verify your account.
  4. Our support team will verify your identity and confirm the deletion request within 7 business days.

Option 2: Request In-App

  1. Open the Happy Foods app and go to Profile > Settings.
  2. Tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm your request by verifying the OTP sent to your registered mobile number.
  4. Your account will be scheduled for deletion once confirmed.

What Data Is Deleted

Once your account deletion request is verified and processed, the following data is permanently deleted:

  • Name and profile information
  • Phone number and email address
  • Saved delivery addresses
  • Saved payment preferences (excluding data held by third-party payment processors)
  • App usage and device data linked to your account

What Data Is Retained, and For How Long

Some data may be retained for a limited period even after account deletion, where required for legal, accounting, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution purposes:

Data Type Retained? Retention Period
Order history & transaction records Yes Up to 7 years (for tax, accounting, and legal compliance)
Payment transaction records Yes (held by payment gateway partners) As required by applicable financial regulations
Fraud-prevention and security logs Yes Up to 90 days
Profile, contact, and location data No Deleted immediately upon confirmed request

After the applicable retention period, retained records are either permanently deleted or anonymized so that they can no longer be linked to you.

Need Help?

If you have questions about account deletion or data retention, contact us at support@happyfoods.com.
